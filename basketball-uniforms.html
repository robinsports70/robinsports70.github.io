Custom Basketball Uniforms, Made to Your Design

Robin Sports is a custom basketball uniforms manufacturer for clubs, schools, academies and AAU programmes. Every uniform is made to order from your colours and crest: jerseys and shorts are fully sublimated, so the design — team name, player names, numbers and sponsor logos — is printed into the fabric itself and will not crack, peel or fade through a season of games and washes. Whether you are kitting out one youth squad or a whole academy, every player runs onto the court in the same sharp, matching kit.

Jerseys, shorts and warm-ups are produced together so your club colours stay consistent across the whole set. Sample first. We make a sample uniform for your approval before the team's full order goes into production. Every size covered. Youth and adult sizes are graded from the same design, so your youngest squad and your senior team look like one club. More than basketball. The same sublimation line produces soccer, baseball and multi-sport kits — see our full products range.

Fabrics and decoration

Game kits are made in breathable, moisture-wicking polyester fabrics — mesh and interlock options that stay light through four quarters. Full sublimation covers the complete garment, including gradients, patterns and sponsor blocks, while crests can also be embroidered on warm-ups and tracksuits for a premium finish. Reversible sets give you a home and away look in a single garment, which keeps a squad's kit bag simple. Our services page explains how sublimation, embroidery and printing compare.

Built for seasons, not just one tournament

A basketball programme does not stand still: new players join, sponsors change and younger squads move up. Because your approved design, colours and size grading are kept on file, adding replacement jerseys or a fresh set for a new age group is straightforward — the new pieces match the kit your team already wears. Many academies also order matching training tees, warm-up tracksuits and coaches' polos from the same design, so the whole programme presents one identity at games, in training and in photos.

What to send us to start your basketball uniform order

A clear brief gets your sample right the first time. Send us: (1) your crest or logo file — a clear image works, a vector file is best; (2) your club colours, with references if you have them; (3) your roster — each player's name, number and size; (4) how many sets you need and which sizes; (5) the date your season or tournament starts, so the sample and the team order are planned around it; (6) anything else in the set — warm-up tops, shooting shirts, training tees or coaches' polos. If you already have a design or a reference photo of a kit you like, send that too — one picture tells us more than a long description. Our guide to ordering full team kits walks through the same steps for any sport.

Sublimation, tackle twill or embroidery — which decoration for basketball?

Most basketball jerseys are fully sublimated: the whole design — team name, numbers, patterns and sponsor logos — is printed into the fabric, so nothing peels or cracks and the jersey stays light. Tackle twill gives numbers and names a stitched, layered look, popular on classic and throwback-style jerseys. Embroidery suits crests and small logos on warm-up hoodies, tracksuits and coaches' polos, where a raised, textured finish looks premium. A single order can mix them: sublimated game jerseys, embroidered warm-ups and printed training tees from the same design. Our services page explains how sublimation, embroidery and printing compare.

Sizing: youth, adult and AAU size runs

Basketball squads mix ages and builds, so we grade every design across youth and adult sizes — the same colours, crest and numbers from the youngest squad to the senior team. When you send your roster, include each player's size; if you are unsure, measure chest and body length on a jersey that fits well now and send those two numbers. Order a spread that covers your whole roster plus one or two spare sets for late sign-ups. Because your approved design and size grading stay on file, replacement jerseys later in the season match the kit your team already wears.

Can every jersey have a different name and number? Yes. Send your roster list with names, numbers and sizes, and each uniform is made for that player.

Can you match our existing club colours and crest? Yes. Send your crest file and colour references and we match them on the sample for your approval.

Do you make youth sizes as well as adult? Yes — youth and adult sizes are made from the same design, so the whole club matches.

Can we start with one sample set? Yes. We recommend approving a sample uniform before the full team order is produced.

When should we order for the season? Order as early in the pre-season as you can: the sample is made and approved first, then the full team order. Sending your brief before the season rush means your kit is ready for the first game, not still in production.
