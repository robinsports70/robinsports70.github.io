Questions Brands Ask

        
FAQ

        
Anything else you need to know? Message us on WhatsApp and we will walk you through it.

        
Ask on WhatsApp

      

      

        
Do I need a tech pack to start?
No. A tech pack helps, but a reference photo, a sample garment or a clear description of the fit and fabric you want is enough for us to develop your first sample.

        
Can I check the quality before ordering in bulk?
Yes — we always start with a sample, so you can approve the fabric, fit, stitching and decoration in your hands before committing to a full run.

        
Can I put my own labels and packaging on the products?
Yes — private label is the core of what we do. Your woven labels, hang tags and packaging go on every piece, with nothing of ours on the finished product.

        
Which decoration methods do you offer?
Embroidery, chenille patches, appliqué, screen printing, DTG, sublimation, foil print and tackle twill — all in-house, and they can be mixed on a single garment.

        
Do you ship worldwide?
Yes. Finished orders are packed and shipped worldwide to brands, clubs, schools and academies.

        
How do I start?
Build your piece in the design studio above and send us the spec sheet, message us on WhatsApp, or DM 'CUSTOM' on Instagram @robinsports70 — whichever is easiest for you.
