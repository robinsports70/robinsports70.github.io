Custom School Leavers Hoodies, Made Simply

Robin Sports is a custom school leavers hoodies manufacturer working with schools, PTAs and academies in the UK, Australia and worldwide. A leavers hoodie is the one school garment students actually keep — so it has to be right. We make each run to your school's design: the crest embroidered or printed on the front, the leaving year in large numbers on the back, and every student's name worked into the back design exactly as your list spells it. Hoodies are packed per student or per class, so hand-out day takes minutes, not hours.

How it works for schools

Send your list. Give us your name list with sizes — a simple spreadsheet is perfect — plus your crest and school colours. Approve the design. We prepare a design proof showing the full name list laid out, so the school can check every spelling before anything is printed. Approve a sample. A sample hoodie lets you check fabric, fit and print quality before the full run. Packed for hand-out. Finished hoodies are packed per student or per class, clearly labelled, ready to distribute. If your school also needs team kits or staff wear, our products page covers the full range.

Styles and options

Choose pullover, full-zip or quarter-zip hoodies in unisex and fitted cuts, in colours matched to your school. Crests can be embroidered for a premium finish or printed to match the back design, and individual names can be added to each student's own hoodie as well as in the shared class list. The same production line makes our custom hoodies for brands, so quality is retail standard, not promotional standard.

More than the leavers run

Most schools come back for more than one order. The same crest file and colour record we keep for your leavers hoodies can be used for school trip hoodies, society and club hoodies, sports tour kits and staff wear, so everything your school orders looks like it belongs together. PTAs also order branded hoodies and tees for fundraising events and fairs. If a student joins late or a size needs changing, small top-up orders can be made against the same approved design, packed and labelled the same way as the main run.

Questions schools ask us

Can every hoodie carry a different student's name? Yes. Names are printed per garment from your list, and the design proof lets you check every name before production.

Can you work from our spreadsheet of names and sizes? Yes — that is exactly how most schools send their order, and we pack the finished hoodies to match it.

Can staff order matching embroidered hoodies or polos? Yes. Staff versions with an embroidered crest are made in the same colours, and we keep your colours and crest file on record so reorders match.

Can we see a sample first? Yes. We recommend approving a sample hoodie before the full year-group run is produced.

How do we start? Send your crest, colours and rough numbers through our contact page, or DM 'CUSTOM' on Instagram @robinsports70.
