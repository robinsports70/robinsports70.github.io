A Custom T-Shirt Manufacturer Built for Clothing Brands

Starting a t-shirt line is hard when big factories only want big orders, and print shops only decorate blanks someone else made. Robin Sports is a private label t-shirt manufacturer for clothing brands — we cut and sew t-shirts from fabric to your design, so your fit, fabric and finish are yours alone. Send a tech pack, a reference photo or even a rough idea, and we develop it into a tee your customers will judge as a retail product, not a promotional giveaway.

Why clothing brands choose Robin Sports

Your label, everywhere it matters. Woven neck labels, printed care labels, hang tags and branded packaging are all done in-house, so your t-shirts arrive ready to sell under your name only. Decoration under one roof. Screen printing, DTG, foil print and embroidery are handled by our own team, which keeps colours and placement consistent from the first piece to the last. Sample first, always. We make a sample for your approval before any bulk production, so you can check the fit, fabric weight and print quality in your hands. Built for growing brands. Start with the styles that define your brand, then scale the winners — your approved patterns and colour references are kept on file so reorders match.

Customisation options

Choose classic crew neck, oversized, fitted or long sleeve fits, in 100% combed cotton jersey from 160 to 220 GSM, or cotton and polyester blends for extra durability. Colours can be matched to your brand palette, and finishes include garment wash and vintage effects. Add screen printed graphics, DTG artwork, foil print, puff print or embroidered logos — every detail is confirmed on your sample before bulk begins. Our services page explains each decoration method in more detail, and our t-shirts guide walks through fabrics, fits and print choices.

Questions buyers ask us

Can I put my own labels and packaging on the t-shirts? Yes — private label is the core of what we do. Your woven labels, hang tags and packaging go on every piece, with nothing of ours on the finished product.

Do I need a tech pack to start? No. A tech pack helps, but a reference photo, a sample garment or a clear description of the fit and fabric you want is enough for us to develop your first sample.

Can I check the quality before ordering in bulk? Yes. We always recommend starting with a sample so you can approve the fabric, fit and print quality before committing to a full run — including a wash test for shrinkage and print durability.

Which print method is best for my design? Screen printing suits bold graphics and larger runs, DTG suits full-colour photographic artwork, foil print adds a metallic shine for streetwear, and embroidery suits small logos with a premium feel. Tell us your artwork and we will recommend the method that fits it best.
