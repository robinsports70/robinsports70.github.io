Custom Polo Shirts Manufacturer — Embroidered Polos with Your Logo & Private Label

As a custom polo shirts manufacturer, Robin Sports works with small brands, clubs, teams, companies and schools that want a polo that looks smart, feels comfortable and carries their identity well. A good polo sits between casual and formal: neat enough for work or an event, and relaxed enough to wear all day. That is why it is a popular choice for uniforms, teamwear, staff clothing, school wear and branded merchandise.

Fabric & Fit

Start with fabric and fit, because they decide how the polo feels and how long it lasts. Classic piqué cotton is breathable and has the textured look most people expect from a polo. Cotton blends can add softness and help the shirt keep its shape, while performance fabrics suit sports teams and active days. A regular fit works well for most people, a slim fit gives a neater shape, and a relaxed fit is more comfortable for all-day wear.

Embroidery & Print for Your Logo

Embroidery is the most popular choice for a logo on a polo. It looks neat, lasts well through washing and gives a professional finish. It works best for simple, clear logos and text. Printing is a useful alternative for larger designs, detailed artwork or a softer feel on the fabric. Look at your logo at the size it will actually appear: small text and fine detail can be hard to read, so a clean version of your artwork usually gives the best result. Share your logo file and say where you would like it placed, and check the placement before the full order is made.

Private Label, Colour & Style

Private label is what turns a plain polo into your own product. You can add your own woven labels, printed neck labels and hang tags, so the shirt carries your brand name instead of a generic label. This matters most for small brands selling their own clothing, and for clubs and companies that want a consistent, professional look. Classic colours such as white, black, navy and grey are easy to match and suit most uniforms, while brighter colours help a team or brand stand out. You can also choose the collar style, placket, buttons and sleeve cuffs.

How Ordering Works

Before you order in full, ask for a sample first. A sample lets you check the fabric, fit, colour, logo size and label in real life, and make changes while they are still easy to make. To order, share your design, colours, sizes and the number of shirts you need, along with your logo and label details.
