Custom Varsity Jacket Manufacturer — Wool & Leather Team Jackets Made for Your Brand

As a custom varsity jacket manufacturer, Robin Sports makes wool and leather team jackets designed around your brand, not a ready-made template. A varsity jacket is easy to recognize: a warm wool body, genuine leather sleeves, ribbed cuffs and collar, and bold detailing that tells a story at a glance. It began on the sports field, but today it belongs just as much on campus, on stage and on the street.

Who Orders Varsity Jackets

Schools, sports teams and clubs order varsity jackets to celebrate achievement and build belonging. A letterman jacket marks a season, a graduation, a championship or years of commitment, and it is worn with pride long after the final whistle. Streetwear brands order them for a different reason: the shape is timeless, the panels offer generous space for artwork, and a well-made jacket can anchor a whole collection.

Customisation & Private Label

Every detail can be customized. Choose your body and sleeve colors, contrast trims, lining and snap buttons, then add chenille patches, embroidery and appliqué to carry your crest, mascot, name, number or slogan. Private labeling puts your brand inside the jacket as well, so the finished piece looks and feels like your own product from the first fitting to the final stitch.

Sample First

Before committing to a bulk order, we always recommend sampling first. A sample lets you check the fit, feel the wool and leather, see your colors and artwork in real life, and confirm that every patch and stitch sits exactly where it should. It is a simple step that removes guesswork, helps you refine the design, and gives you confidence before production begins.

Start Your Design

Ready to design yours? Explore more from Robin Sports on our website and follow us on Instagram for recent styles, design ideas and behind-the-scenes updates. Share your colors, artwork and quantity, and we will help you turn the idea into a jacket your team or customers will want to wear again and again.
