How to Find a Custom Tracksuit Manufacturer for Your Brand

Finding a custom tracksuit manufacturer for your brand is the step that decides whether your tracksuits look like a proper collection or a mismatched set. A full tracksuit — a matching jacket and pants in your colours, with your logo, print or embroidery — has to be made as one set, not bought piece by piece. At Robin Sports, we make custom tracksuits for brands, clubs and schools across the USA, UK, Europe and Australia, with embroidery, print, sublimation and private label done in-house.

What should I ask a custom tracksuit manufacturer before ordering?

Ask what the manufacturer makes in-house. A real tracksuit manufacturer cuts, sews and finishes its own sets, so it can change the fit, the fabric and the details. Ask how the jacket and pants are matched as a set, how your logo is applied, and what you need to send to get a sample made first.

Also ask about the details that make a tracksuit feel finished: zips that run smoothly, cuffs and waistbands that hold their shape, pockets placed where you want them, and colours matched exactly between the jacket and the pants. A manufacturer that handles the whole set keeps every piece in the same colours and the same fabric, so the tracksuit looks like one product, not two separate garments.

What tracksuit fabrics and GSM should I choose for my brand?

Choose the fabric by how the tracksuit will be worn. Fleece and French terry are the usual choice for everyday and streetwear sets, in a midweight to heavyweight range that feels warm without being bulky. Nylon and woven shells suit sport and training sets. Ask your manufacturer which fabric weight fits your market and your season.

The jacket and pants should be cut from the same fabric batch so the colour matches exactly — ask for this specifically. A good manufacturer will also tell you how the fabric washes, because a tracksuit that shrinks or fades after a few washes will cost you customers. Feel the sample, wash it once, and check the colour and the fit before you approve the bulk order.

Embroidery, print or sublimation — which decoration for tracksuits?

Embroidery suits chest logos and small crests on fleece tracksuits, where a raised, durable finish looks best. Print works for bold graphics and large back designs on both fleece and nylon. Sublimation is for all-over colour and patterns on polyester shells, because the design is printed into the fabric and cannot crack or peel.

Tell your manufacturer what your design looks like and where it should sit, and ask which method they recommend for each spot. Most brands mix them — an embroidered logo on the chest with a printed design on the back. Check the decoration on the sample: a print that looks fine on screen can sit very differently on a real jacket, and embroidery on a thin shell fabric can pucker if it is not done right.

How do I check tracksuit quality before a bulk order?

Always start with a sample set, not just a fabric swatch. Check that the jacket and pants match in colour, that the zip runs smoothly, the cuffs and waistband hold their shape, and the print or embroidery sits where you asked. Wash the sample once to check shrinkage and colour, then approve the sample before the bulk order is cut.

Try the sample on and move in it — a tracksuit has to feel right when you walk, sit and stretch, not just when it hangs on a rail. Check the pockets, the drawcords and the stitching at the knees and elbows, where wear shows first. A sample you can hold is worth more than any photo.

Can a manufacturer add my private label to tracksuits?

Yes. Private label means the tracksuit carries your brand name, not the factory's. Your woven neck labels, printed care labels, hang tags and packaging go on every piece, so the sets arrive ready to sell as your own. Your label is sewn in during production, not added afterwards, and the same labels go on the jacket and the pants.

For brands, the label is part of the product — it is what your customer sees first. Send your label artwork with your order and the manufacturer will place it exactly where you want it, on every piece of the set.

What do I send a tracksuit manufacturer to start an order?

Send a clear brief: the style you want — zip-up or pullover jacket, joggers or straight-leg pants — a sketch or reference photo, your colours and logo file, the sizes you need, and your quantities per style and colour. A tech pack with measurements and label positions makes the sample right the first time.

If you only have an idea, a good manufacturer will help you shape it into a workable design. Share what you have, and ask what else they need — the back and forth at this stage is what keeps the sample on track. A season date also helps the manufacturer plan the order.

If you are ready to make tracksuits for your brand, club or school, start with a conversation. Share your design or idea, and we will help you shape it into a set worth wearing.
