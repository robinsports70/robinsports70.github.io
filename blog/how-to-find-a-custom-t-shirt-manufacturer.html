How to Find a Custom T-Shirt Manufacturer for Your Brand

Finding a custom t-shirt manufacturer for your brand is the step that decides whether your first collection looks like a retail product or a giveaway. The right manufacturer cuts and sews the shirt to your design — your fabric, your fit, your labels — instead of selling you a blank tee with a logo added at the end. What should I ask a custom t-shirt manufacturer before ordering?

Ask what the manufacturer actually controls. A real custom manufacturer sources the fabric, makes the patterns, cuts, sews and decorates in-house, so it can change the shirt itself. Ask to see real process photos, a measurement chart and fabric specs with real GSM numbers, and ask how a sample is handled and what happens if the bulk order does not match it.

What t-shirt fabrics and GSM should I choose for my brand?

For a soft everyday tee, 100% combed cotton jersey at 160 to 180 GSM is the usual starting point. For a heavier streetwear tee, look at 180 to 220 GSM. Cotton and polyester blends add durability and hold a print well, and a heavier fabric drapes better on oversized fits. Always ask for fabric swatches before you commit, and check the weight on your sample.

Screen print vs embroidery vs DTG — which is best for my design?

Screen printing suits bold graphics and larger runs, and it stays bright wash after wash. Embroidery suits small chest logos and a premium feel. DTG (direct-to-garment) suits full-colour photographic artwork on smaller runs. Sublimation suits all-over prints on polyester. A good manufacturer offers all of them and tells you honestly which one fits your artwork.

How do I check t-shirt quality before a bulk order?

Always start with a sample. Check the fabric hand-feel, the stitching strength at the seams, the print durability, the colour accuracy and the shrinkage after one wash. Measure the sample against the size chart you approved, and look at the finishing — clean neck ribbing, straight hems and no loose threads. A sample you can hold is worth more than any photo.

Can a manufacturer add my private label to t-shirts?

Yes. Private label means the shirt carries your name, not the factory's. Your woven neck labels, printed care labels, hang tags and packaging go on every piece, so the shirts arrive ready to sell as part of your own collection.
