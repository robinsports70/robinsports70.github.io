A Custom Soccer Uniforms Manufacturer Built for Clubs and Schools

A soccer kit is the most visible thing a club owns — it is on every player, in every photo, at every match. Robin Sports is a custom soccer uniforms manufacturer for clubs, academies and schools across the USA, UK, Europe and Australia. We cut and sew soccer uniforms from fabric to your design, so your colours, crest and fit are yours alone — not a catalogue blank with a logo ironed on. Send a tech pack, a reference photo or even a rough idea, and we develop it into a kit your players will be proud to wear. Why clubs choose Robin Sports

Sublimation that lasts. Your colours, crest, names and numbers are printed into the fabric itself, so they never crack, peel or fade — even after a full season of washing. Customisation options

Choose lightweight polyester jerseys in 150 to 180 GSM for match days, with mesh panels for extra breathability, or heavier training fabrics for winter sessions. Colours are matched to your club palette, and finishes include sublimated all-over designs, tackle twill numbers and embroidered crests. Add your sponsor, player names and numbers, and your own labels and packaging. Our services page explains sublimation and embroidery in more detail, and our team kits guide walks through fabrics, fits and decoration choices. Questions buyers ask us

Can I put my club crest and sponsor on the jerseys? Yes — your crest, sponsor logo, player names and numbers are all part of the design. Send your artwork files and we will place them exactly where you want them.

What sizes do you cover? Youth and adult size runs, from the youngest academy players to the first team. Send your roster and we will size the order to it.

Can I check the quality before ordering in bulk? Yes. We always recommend starting with a sample so you can approve the fabric, fit and print quality before committing to a full run — including a wash test for colour and print durability.

Sublimation or tackle twill — which is better for soccer? Sublimation suits full-colour designs and names and numbers that never peel. Tackle twill suits a classic stitched look for numbers and names. Tell us your design and we will recommend the method that fits it best.

How do I start? Send your crest, colours and roster through our contact page, or DM 'CUSTOM' on Instagram @robinsports70. You can also read our guide on custom team kits first.
